A strong Civilathon submission should tell a complete engineering story. Do not begin with technology first. Begin with the problem, prove that it matters and then show why your solution is worth considering.

The recommended thought process

01 · PROBLEM

Define the real issue

What exactly is going wrong? Who is affected? Where does the problem occur? What happens if it remains unsolved?

02 · RESEARCH

Understand the existing scenario

Study existing methods, standards, papers, products, practices or solutions. Identify limitations, gaps and opportunities for improvement.

03 · SOLUTION

Explain your proposed approach

Describe the concept step by step. A reader should understand what your system, method, material or process does and how it will be used.

04 · INNOVATION

Show what is different

Explain the specific improvement over existing approaches: faster, safer, cheaper, greener, more accurate, more accessible or more scalable.

05 · FEASIBILITY

Think like an engineer

Discuss materials, equipment, software, manpower, implementation steps, approximate cost, limitations and practical constraints.

06 · IMPACT

Measure the benefit

Explain how the idea can reduce time, cost, waste, risk or emissions—or improve safety, quality, productivity, resilience or public benefit.

What we expect from your team

  • Clear and evidence-based problem identification.
  • Meaningful research rather than generic statements.
  • Technical reasoning appropriate to the selected domain.
  • A solution that is understandable, practical and implementable.
  • Honest assumptions and clearly stated limitations.
  • A concise, professional and visually clear presentation.
