{{ title }}

Please read the complete rules before preparing your idea and PPT. The final registration portal will require participants to confirm that they have understood these guidelines.

01

Eligibility & participation

Participation may be individual or in a team of up to 4 members. Each participant/team must provide accurate contact and college details during registration.

02

Originality

Ideas must be original work of the participating student/team. Existing technologies may be studied or referenced, but copied submissions, fabricated claims or unattributed work may be rejected.

03

One submission

Each registration should represent one primary idea. Teams should select the most appropriate research domain and clearly identify the problem being addressed.

04

PPT submission

The final presentation must be submitted through the official portal in the format and within the deadline announced by the organisers. Once the final submission window closes, changes may not be accepted.

05

Professional conduct

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully with organisers, coordinators, judges and fellow participants. Misconduct may lead to disqualification.

06

Evaluation

Ideas may be evaluated on problem relevance, research, innovation, technical approach, feasibility, impact, clarity of presentation and overall engineering value.

07

Use of AI and external resources

AI tools and online resources may be used for research, ideation or assistance where appropriate, but teams remain responsible for the accuracy, originality and understanding of their final work. Do not present generated content as research without verification.

08

Jury decision

The decision of the jury and organising committee regarding shortlisting, ranking and awards will be final, subject to the event rules.

Event schedule: Date, time and venue will be announced soon.
