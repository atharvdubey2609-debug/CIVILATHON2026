CIVILATHON

{{ title }}

Civilathon is an inter-college civil engineering innovation challenge created to encourage students to look at the built environment differently: observe a problem, understand why it exists, research what has already been attempted and then design a solution with real-world value.

More than a competition

The event is intended as a platform for engineering thinking. We are not looking only for complicated technology or expensive prototypes. We are looking for ideas that demonstrate a clear understanding of a problem and a thoughtful path towards solving it.

Participants can work on construction management, sustainable infrastructure, materials, transportation, water resources, structures, surveying, smart cities, AI/ML, IoT, GIS and other relevant areas. Interdisciplinary ideas are welcome when the civil engineering problem and expected impact are clearly established.

What makes a strong Civilathon idea?

Problem clarity

The problem is specific, genuine and supported by observations, data, research or a clear existing gap.

Engineering reasoning

The proposed approach follows sound civil engineering principles and explains how the solution would actually work.

Innovation

The idea introduces a meaningful improvement in method, material, process, technology, monitoring, safety, sustainability or decision-making.

Feasibility & impact

The team considers resources, cost, implementation, scalability and the measurable benefit created by the solution.

Prototype is not compulsory

A working hardware or software prototype is not compulsory. A well-researched concept, supported by methodology, technical reasoning, feasibility and expected impact, can be submitted through the PPT route. If a prototype, simulation, model or demonstration strengthens the idea, participants are encouraged to include it.
