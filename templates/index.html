THE CHALLENGE

Turn real problems into real solutions.

Civilathon is built to give students a platform to move beyond classroom theory. Participants are encouraged to observe challenges around construction and infrastructure, research the existing scenario, identify gaps and propose solutions that are technically sound, feasible and capable of creating measurable impact.

01 · IDENTIFY

Find a problem worth solving

Start with a genuine civil engineering challenge. It may come from construction sites, transportation, water management, structures, surveying, sustainability, urban infrastructure or any emerging technology.

02 · INNOVATE

Develop an engineering solution

Use research, design thinking, engineering principles and technology to develop a solution. A working prototype is not compulsory; a well-researched and practical concept is equally welcome.

03 · IMPACT

Show why it can matter

Explain feasibility, implementation, resources, cost considerations, scalability and the technical, environmental, social or economic impact your solution could create.

WHY PARTICIPATE?

Your idea can become the next engineering solution.

Research-driven

Build your proposal on observations, literature, data, existing practices and clearly identified gaps.

Practical by design

We value ideas that can realistically be implemented, tested, improved or scaled in the real world.

Open to innovation

Use civil engineering fundamentals with AI, GIS, IoT, automation, materials or other relevant technologies.

Impact focused

Think about safety, time, cost, sustainability, productivity, resilience and the people who benefit from your solution.
