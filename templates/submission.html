The PPT is the primary format through which your idea will be evaluated. Use it to communicate your engineering thinking, not simply to decorate the idea.

Recommended PPT structure

01Title & TeamIdea title, team name, members and college.
02Problem StatementDefine the real-world problem and why it matters.
03Existing ScenarioCurrent methods, limitations and research gap.
04Research & EvidenceRelevant literature, observations, data or references.
05Proposed SolutionExplain the concept, components and working.
06MethodologyShow the process, workflow, architecture or implementation.
07InnovationClearly state what makes your approach different.
08FeasibilityCost, resources, implementation and scalability.
09ImpactTechnical, social, environmental and economic benefits.
10Future Scope & ReferencesNext steps, limitations and sources used.

File requirements

  • Submit the final presentation as PPT or PPTX.
  • Keep slides concise, readable and technically meaningful.
  • Use diagrams, flowcharts, graphs, drawings or screenshots where they improve understanding.
  • Clearly mention assumptions, data sources and references.
  • Final slide count, maximum file size, naming format and deadline will be announced on the official portal before submissions open.
Important: A working prototype is not compulsory. A strong concept supported by research, methodology, feasibility and expected impact can be submitted as a PPT.
